### [2026-10-14] Feature: glasses glance, notifications and gateway transcription

The WednesdayAI miniapp for MentraOS display glasses shows a glance when you tilt your head up, and shows notifications from apps you choose on Android, one at a time and paged. It also transcribes the question after the wake word on your WednesdayAI gateway, falling back to Mentra's transcript.

**Benefits:** hands-free glance at time and status; chosen notifications on the glasses; more accurate question transcription through your own gateway.
